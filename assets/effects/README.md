# Original sky-play sound effects

`pop.mp3` and `kite.mp3` are original synthesized effects generated for this app by `scripts/generate-sky-effects.py`. No third-party samples, voices, or animal recordings are used.

The pop is approximately 0.21 seconds and the kite chime approximately 0.4 seconds. Both are deliberately short and quiet; playback is also volume-limited. They are separate from the licensed human-recording catalogue and work offline without a text-to-speech voice.

To regenerate, use Python 3 and FFmpeg. Review volume on the target tablet before distributing an APK.
